02 · About the Project

A personalized fitness & health tracking experience.

Fitnpulse is designed around clarity, consistency and motivation — steps, heart rate, calories, water, sleep and stress live together in one calm dashboard, with AI Insights that turn raw numbers into next actions.

ProjectFitnpuls Case Study
RoleUI/UX Designer
TypePersonal Project
ScopeResearch → React
1
The goalMake daily health tracking feel effortless — one glance shows where you are, one tap shows what to do next.
2
The approachResearch → persona → IA → flows → wireframes → visual design → a working React build of every screen.
3
The systemOutfit + DM Sans type, a token-driven color ramp, glass surfaces and one component kit shared by all 30 screens.
4
The resultTwo complete themes, motion that rewards progress, and a case study that documents every decision end to end.

03 · Typography

One family, every job.

The product pairs Outfit (display + UI emphasis) with DM Sans (body + captions) — the same pairing declared in the real app stylesheet. Tight tracking on display sizes keeps numbers and headlines compact inside phone widths.

04 · Motion · Micro-interactions

Micro-interactions that reward progress.

The exact demo set from the main case study — every micro-interaction runs live with the same timing curves as the shipped app.

Logo pop — first launch

Sets the brand tone at the very first touchpoint.

Staggered bar rise — dashboard

Assembles the weekly rhythm so the eye reads the data in order.

Count-up — step total

0steps today · counting on view

Makes the headline number feel earned — attention lands on the metric.

Ring glide — daily goal

82%

Shows goal progress as motion, reinforcing the daily target.

Chart fill — progress trend

Reveals the trend direction — improvement becomes visible, not implied.

Theme morph — light / dark

Proves the tokens adapt the whole interface — one design, two modes.

05 · Color System

Tokens, not hexes.

Every surface references --fp-* theme tokens, so dark and light modes stay in sync. Accents follow the real product ramp: emerald for health, cyan for sync/info, lime/orange/red/purple for states and charts.

Background--fp-bgApp canvas gradient (light)
Surface / Glass--fp-glassCards, sheets, nav blur
Primary Text--fp-textHeadlines, metric numerals
Muted Text--fp-mutedSubs, captions, timestamps
Border--fp-borderCard + input hairlines
Emerald Accent--fp-accentPrimary CTA, active tabs, goals
Cyan Accent--fp-accent-2Sync, info, links, charts
Gradient Tokensbrand rampLogo, streak rings, hero glows
Lime--fp-limeSuccess, low stress, PRs
Orange--fp-orangeStreaks, warnings, challenges
Red--fp-redHeart-rate, destructive actions
Purple--fp-purpleSleep, community highlights
06 · Dark + Light Theme

One interface, two visual modes.

Identical layout and content — only --fp-* tokens change: background, glass surfaces, text, muted text, borders, accents and glows. The real app persists the choice and crossfades surfaces over ~420ms.

07 · Splash Screen

Brand first, then momentum.

The real splash holds ~800ms: logo scales in (250ms), wordmark + tagline stagger up, then a thin loading ring spins while the app hydrates — before auto-advancing to walkthrough.

08 · Walkthrough

Three promises, one swipe.

Three onboarding slides sell the core loop — track, AI insights, community — with directional slide transitions (forward slides right, back slides left) and an expanding-dot progress indicator.

09 · Onboarding

Setup that earns the data.

Four setup stages — profile, device sync, goals, permissions — each explain what the user gives, what the app returns, and why it matters.

Step 01
🧑‍🦶

Profile Setup

Name, age, height, weight, gender + fitness level (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced).

Does → fills personal baselineGets → calibrated goals + levelsWhy → personalization from day one
Step 02
⌚

Device Sync

Apple Watch, Galaxy, Fitbit, Garmin, Mi Band or generic BLE — with live searching → found → syncing states.

Does → taps a tracker to pairGets → progress % + connected glowWhy → complete health sync, no manual logs
Step 03
🎯

Goal Selection

Lose Weight, Build Muscle, Stay Active, Improve Cardio, Healthy Lifestyle — multi-select chips.

Does → picks what mattersGets → selected cards scale + glowWhy → the home feed adapts to intent
Step 04
📱

Permissions

Notifications, Health Data, Location, Bluetooth — real iOS-style toggles, skippable.

Does → flips honest togglesGets → Finish Setup celebrationWhy → full experience, user stays in control
